About

The strategist
behind the work

Toluwani Hephzibah Alade is a Culturally Intuitive Brand and Media Strategist who thinks like a challenger. With 6 years of experience across branding, media, and strategic storytelling, she leads strategy direction at QVT Media, shaping how brands think, communicate, and compete in saturated markets.

Her approach: interrogate the norm, uncover what others overlook, and translate insights into bold, emotionally intelligent ideas that actually move people.

A forward thinker, Toluwani actively integrates AI into her strategic practice and teaches young professionals in media and marketing how to do the same with intention and clarity. She is also the founder of City on a Hill (COH), where she has trained over 100 young professionals in career strategy and growth.
